⚠ Ideal potential flow — no viscosity, no separation, no stall. Real wings stall when the boundary layer separates (typically α ≈ 12–18°).

WebGL note: the pressure field is evaluated per-pixel in a fragment shader (inverse Joukowski + complex velocity + chain rule), so it is full-resolution and recomputed every frame on the GPU. Streamlines, particles and the Cp plot are drawn on a 2D overlay canvas.
